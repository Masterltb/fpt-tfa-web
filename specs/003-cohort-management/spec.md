# 003: Cohort & Project Management

## Business Requirements
- Lecturers must be able to create a new Cohort (Class) with a name.
- Lecturers must be able to list all Cohorts they own.
- Lecturers must be able to add Projects within a Cohort (defining min/max team size and required roles).
- This fulfills the BRD requirement: "Thiết lập lớp/dự án và tiêu chí nhóm bởi giảng viên".

## User Experience
- In the React frontend, there will be a new "Dashboard" screen for lecturers.
- It displays a list of their cohorts.
- A button to "Create Cohort" opens a small form.
- Clicking on a cohort goes to a "Cohort Details" page where they can manage projects and trigger the "Formation Console" without manually typing the Cohort ID.
